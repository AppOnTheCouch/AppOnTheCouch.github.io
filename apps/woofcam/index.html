🔍 How it works

1

Give your old phone a new purpose

Dig out that old smartphone from the drawer. WoofCam turns it into a live pet camera — for free.

2

Set it up in seconds

Sign in with your free WoofCam account and point the camera phone at your dog's, cat's, or any pet's favorite spot.

3

Watch live from anywhere

See your pet in real time from your main phone, whether you're at the office or on the go.

✨ Free vs Premium

🆓 Free

  • Turn any old phone into a live pet camera
  • Watch the live stream from your main phone, anywhere
  • Works great for dogs, cats, and any companion animal

⭐ Premium

  • Motion & sound detection alerts, sent straight to your phone
  • One-tap local recording, saved directly on your device
  • An affordable upgrade — no big investment required
🚫 No clutter, no annoying ads. WoofCam stays simple — and Premium won't cost you a fortune.
