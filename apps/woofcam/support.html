Frequently asked questions

Which phones can I use as a camera?

Most old smartphones with a working camera and internet connection (Wi-Fi is enough) can be used as a WoofCam camera device.

Do I need two phones?

Yes — one older phone acts as the camera, placed near your pet, and your main phone is used to watch the live stream.

What's included in Premium?

Premium unlocks motion & sound detection alerts and one-tap local recording, saved directly to your device. It's an affordable upgrade — WoofCam isn't cluttered with ads and Premium won't cost you a fortune.

Does WoofCam work for cats too?

Absolutely — WoofCam works for dogs, cats, and any other companion animal you'd like to keep an eye on.
